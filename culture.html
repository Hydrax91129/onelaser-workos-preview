四大核心价值观Our Four Core Values

01

每天进步一点Learn & Get Better Every Day

Learn & Get Better Every Day每天进步一点

公司成长的速度，取决于每个人成长的速度。OneLaser can only grow as fast as our people grow.

  • 产品：每一代、每一版，都比上一次更好。Products: every generation, every release beats the last.
  • 人：主动学习新技能、新工具（包括 AI），学了就用。People: keep learning new skills and tools, including AI, and use them.
  • 效率：今天一小时做完的事，明天争取半小时；能自动化就不手工重复。Efficiency: an hour today, thirty minutes tomorrow. If it can be automated, automate it.
  • 复盘：可以犯错，同样的错不犯第二次。Review: mistakes are allowed. Repeating the same one is not.

底线BOTTOM LINE学习能力不足、不愿积极进取的人，不适合 OneLaser。People who can't learn or won't push forward are not a fit for OneLaser.

02

以客户为尊The Customer Is Our Teacher

The Customer Is Our Teacher以客户为尊

客户是我们的老师。Start with the customer and work backwards.Every question, complaint and review teaches us how to get better. Start with the customer and work backwards.

  • 懂客户：客户买的是一门生意、一份创造力。知道他们真正想要什么，并保持敏感和警惕。Know them: customers buy a business and a creative ability. Know what they truly want, and stay alert as it changes.
  • 先问一句：做任何决定前，先问“这对客户有什么好处？”Ask first: before any decision, ask "How does this help the customer?"
  • 亲自去看：看工单原文、上机复现、听客户原话。Go and see: read the original ticket, reproduce it on the machine, hear the customer's own words.
  • 开箱就能出好结果：这就是对客户最大的尊重。Great results out of the box: the highest form of respect for a customer.
  • 说真话：参数真实可复现，绝不虚标，承诺的一定做到。Tell the truth: real, reproducible specs. No inflated claims. We deliver what we promise.
03

创新来自行动与速度Innovation = Action + Speed

Innovation = Action + Speed创新来自行动与速度

创新是做出来的，不是想出来的。速度来自聪明：巧妙找到最优解，最快实施。Innovation is built, not imagined. Speed comes from being smart: find the best solution, then execute it fast.

  • 先行动再完善：小成本快速验证，跑通了再放大。Act, then refine: test small and fast, scale what works.
  • 找最聪明的路：善用已有技术、工具和伙伴，不重复造轮子。Take the smartest path: use existing technology, tools and partners. Don't reinvent the wheel.
  • 用结果说话：汇报结果和数据，忙不等于贡献。Show results: report outcomes and data. Busy is not the same as contributing.
  • 24 小时响应：做不到的事提前说，不拖到截止日。Respond within 24 hours: if you can't deliver, say so early, not at the deadline.

底线BOTTOM LINE没有行动力、执行力的人，天天说空话、放卫星、没有结果的人，不适合 OneLaser。People who don't act, can't execute, or talk big without results are not a fit for OneLaser.

04

坚持最高标准Hold the Highest Standard

Hold the Highest Standard坚持最高标准

It's not done until it's done. 没完成就是没完成，不提前庆祝。It's not done until it's done. No celebrating before the finish line.

  • 三层质量：产品质量、工作质量、交付质量，交付前自己先验收。Three layers of quality: product, work and delivery. Check your own work first.
  • 人人有权叫停：发现质量问题，任何人都可以暂停出货，不会被责怪。Anyone can stop the line: spot a quality issue, hold the shipment, no blame.
  • One Team：语言、文化、种族的差异，不是合作的障碍，而是我们的优势。One Team: differences in language, culture or ethnicity are never a barrier. They are our strength.
  • One Standard：全球团队同一标准、同一数据。会上可以反对，决定后全力执行。One Standard: one standard and one set of data for every team worldwide. Disagree in the meeting, then commit fully.

我们不接受的行为What We Don't Accept

  • 不学习、不进步，停在舒适区Refusing to learn, staying in the comfort zone
  • 被动等指令，看到问题却不管Waiting for instructions, ignoring problems
  • 说空话、放卫星，承诺做不到Empty talk and promises never kept
  • 虚报进度、隐瞒问题、美化数据Misreporting progress, hiding problems, dressing up data
  • 虚标参数，对客户夸大承诺Inflating specs or overpromising to customers
  • 做事不闭环，交一半就当完成Half-finished work called "done"
  • 信息不共享，各自为战Hoarding information, working in silos
  • 因语言或文化差异推卸配合Using language or culture as an excuse not to cooperate
